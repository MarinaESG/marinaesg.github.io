Independent public-source reconstruction · 2026

B Corp impact reporting controls

A public-source reconstruction of the reporting process behind impact disclosures.

The reporting problem

Published impact information can provide useful headline measures without enough detail to re-perform the calculation or confirm the reporting boundary.

The work sample

Using Oxford HR’s 2024 Impact Report as the public source, I reconstructed an impact-reporting control approach covering customers, workforce, community and environment.

The work defines KPI calculation bases, proposed owners and evidence requirements, and records qualifications where comparatives or methodology remain unclear.

The evidence chain

01KPI
02Owner
03Calculation

Reviewable outputs

  • Impact KPI definitions and calculation bases.
  • Proposed reporting ownership and evidence requirements.
  • Reconciliation of disclosed information.
  • Recorded methodology, denominator and comparative gaps.

Why ownership matters

A public-source reconstruction can propose who should own or review an input. It cannot establish that an organisation has appointed those owners or implemented the controls.

Scope and limitations

Oxford HR identifies the source report, not a client relationship or endorsement. This work is not B Lab assurance, certification verification or access to internal company records. Proposed controls are recommendations, not observed internal procedures.
