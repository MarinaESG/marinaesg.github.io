Research · Oxford Brookes University

Corporate disclosure research

Structured interpretation, traceable sources and documented judgement.

Research context

As a Doctoral Researcher at Oxford Brookes University from September 2019 to September 2025, I examined corporate sustainability disclosure, reporting quality and comparability.

The research evidence base covered approximately 500 corporate reports and related disclosure materials, including annual reports, sustainability reports and regulatory disclosures.

A repeatable methodology

I designed and applied a GRI-mapped, four-level coding and scoring framework, with defined classifications, source references and documented judgements.

  • Defined inclusion and classification rules to support consistent interpretation.
  • Investigated missing, ambiguous and non-comparable information.
  • Maintained traceability from source disclosures to item-level assessments and aggregate findings.
  • Used Excel, Atlas.ti and Power BI to organise evidence and compare reporting practices.

Relevance to sustainability reporting

The work developed practical capabilities in evidence synthesis, methodology documentation, data quality and transparent limitations. These are relevant when reporting judgements need to be revisited or challenged.

“Approximately 500” describes corporate reports and related disclosure materials. It is not a count of companies or an audited inventory.

Research award

Master of Philosophy, Oxford Brookes University, awarded August 2025. “Doctoral Researcher” describes the research role; it is not a claim to an awarded PhD.

Scope and limitations

This page summarises the research method, not the full thesis or underlying corpus. The visual diagrams illustrate the methodology and do not represent research findings.
