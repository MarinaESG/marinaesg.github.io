Independent technical work sample

Standards into reporting controls

Translating a disclosure requirement into a structured, reviewable question specification.

The reporting problem

A requirement is difficult to apply consistently when the datapoint, reporting boundary, evidence expectations and validation rules remain implicit.

The prototype approach

The work sample structures a chain from source requirement to datapoint, question, guidance, evidence, validation, exception, reviewer decision and controlled change.

Example decision rules

Missing is not the same as zero

A missing datapoint stays an information gap. Recording zero requires a defined basis and supporting evidence.

A nil adjustment needs support

The reviewer should be able to see the reconciliation and rationale supporting a decision that no adjustment is needed.

Critical exceptions constrain approval

An approved reporting status should not coexist with an unresolved critical exception. The exception, owner and resolution should remain visible.

Reporting relevance

The method connects interpretation to consistent data collection and review. It also makes the assumptions behind a mapping explicit, including where requirements overlap and where they differ.

Scope and limitations

This is a prototype methodology, not an official crosswalk or standard-setter product. It does not represent official CDP content or demonstrate equivalence between reporting frameworks. The source implementation is not attached to this preview.
