Two languages.

Connecting international disclosure requirements with controlled reporting processes across data owners and jurisdictions.

The reporting challenge

When source data, reporting owners and disclosure requirements sit in different jurisdictions, a consistent number depends on shared definitions and a documented method.

My interest is in the reporting-control challenges facing Chinese and China-linked businesses operating internationally: translating the required datapoint into a clear request, retaining the evidence and making exceptions visible to reviewers.

The background I bring

Mandarin & EnglishAccounting & financeChina financial servicesUK disclosure research

My financial-services experience in Shenzhen and Hong Kong involved financial information, client documentation and communication across professional stakeholders. UK research added structured sustainability-disclosure review and methodology development.

A practical reporting chain

  1. Define the disclosure requirement and reporting boundary.
  2. Identify the UK or China data owner and source record.
  3. Document methodology, assumptions and translation choices.
  4. Reconcile the calculation and retain the supporting evidence.
  5. Resolve exceptions and record reviewer approval.

This is a developing specialism informed by my background and independent work. It is not presented as previously delivered cross-border sustainability advisory work.
