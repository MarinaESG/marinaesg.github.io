My translation method

  1. Identify the requirement. Record the authoritative source, version and scope.
  2. Define the datapoint. Specify units, boundary, period and calculation method.
  3. Assign reporting responsibility. Identify the data owner and reviewer.
  4. Design the checks. Define validation, reconciliation and exception handling.
  5. Retain the evidence. Link assumptions and supporting records to the review decision.

Framework familiarity is distinct from delivered implementation experience. The site does not provide regulatory advice, current applicability assessments or a certified crosswalk.
