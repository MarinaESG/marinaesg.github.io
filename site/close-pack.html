Independent portfolio project · 2026

Sustainability reporting close pack

A controlled route from environmental KPI inputs to a documented release decision.

The reporting problem

A complete-looking KPI can still have unresolved evidence, reconciliation or approval gaps. The reporting process needs to expose those gaps before release.

The approach

The independent close-pack workflow covers eight illustrative environmental KPIs across Scope 1, Scope 2, Scope 3 purchased goods and services, energy, water and waste. Each KPI is linked to an owner, source, evidence reference and approval status.

Controls that make a difference

Control design described in the portfolio strategy
ControlReview question
Completeness and unique IDsAre required inputs present, with one identifiable record per KPI?
Numerical and range checksAre values valid, with percentages within the permitted range?
Evidence and datesAre references and dates consistently structured?
Movement reviewHave material changes been explained?
Approval statusAre unresolved exceptions visible to the reviewer?

Reviewable outputs

An exception log and management summary make unresolved issues visible. The reporting design connects findings to follow-up actions and a review decision.

Control score and release status answer different questions. A high score does not authorise release when a critical exception remains unresolved.

Why it matters

The workflow shows how reporting checks can support an accountable close: defined inputs, traceable exceptions and a decision that a reviewer can challenge.

Scope and limitations

Illustrative data and independent portfolio work. This is not an operational client reporting cycle or an assurance opinion. The case study does not assert a current validation score or release status.
